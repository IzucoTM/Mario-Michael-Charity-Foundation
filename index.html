Who We Are?

Mario Michael Charity Foundation (Gboo Nti Nation 🌎) is a nonprofit organization committed to transforming lives and uplifting underserved communities across Nigeria and beyond. We believe that every individual deserves access to basic needs food, education, healthcare, and opportunity. Our mission is to be a source of hope and positive change for those who need it most. Through compassion, transparency, and action, we are building a nation of impact, one life at a time.

OUR MISSION

To empower disadvantaged individuals and communities by providing sustainable support in education, healthcare, food security, and youth development.

OUR VISION

To create a world where no one is left behind — a world where compassion drives change and communities thrive together.

OUR CORE VALUES

  • • Compassion - We serve with love and empathy.
  • • Integrity - Transparency and accountability in everything we do.
  • • Impact - We focus on measurable and lasting change.
  • • Unity - Together, we are stronger.
  • • Service - We exist to uplift others.

our programmes

Education Support

We provide scholarships, school supplies, mentorship programs, and financial assistance to children and young adults who cannot afford quality education.

food outreach

Regular food distribution programs to support families facing hunger and hardship.

Healthcare Assistance

Medical outreach programs, free checkups, medication support, and health awareness campaigns.

Community Empowerment

Skill acquisition training, entrepreneurship support, and leadership development programs for young people.
